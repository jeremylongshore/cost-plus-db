How Flat-Rate Pricing Works

Why not strict cost + 25% on everything?

Good question. Our base tiers use flat-rate pricing, while add-ons use strict cost + 25%. Here's why:

Base Tiers = Flat-Rate (Higher Margins)

Our flat-rate tiers ($59/$119/$179/$299) include MORE than just infrastructure. They include:

  • Base infrastructure - Contabo VPS ($2-30/mo)
  • Management overhead - Setup, monitoring, support, maintenance (not free)
  • Backup infrastructure - Wasabi S3, pgBackRest configuration (~$5/mo)
  • Monitoring tools - Betterstack, alerting ($8/mo)
  • Our time - 4-hour support SLA, incident response, provisioning

Why higher margins on base tiers? Because the value isn't just the VPS - it's the management, support, and expertise. AWS doesn't show you this breakdown. We do.

Add-Ons = Strict Cost + 25%

When you add optional upgrades, those are priced at exact cost + 25% and shown transparently on your invoice:

  • Infrastructure upgrades (Hetzner/DigitalOcean/AWS)
  • High Availability (+$99/mo)
  • VPN Access (+$15/mo)
  • Read Replicas (+$15/mo)
  • Compliance Package (+$100/mo)

You'll see the exact cost we pay on every invoice. No tricks.
