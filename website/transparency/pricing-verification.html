Why Base Tier Margins Are Higher

You might notice our base tiers ($49/$89/$129/$149) have higher margins than 25%. Here's why:

  • VPS cost: $6.50-35/mo (depending on tier)
  • Backup storage: $1-3/mo
  • Monitoring: $1/mo per customer
  • Our management time:
    • Initial setup: 1-2 hours
    • Ongoing support: 4-hour SLA, email responses, incident handling
    • Backup verification: Daily checks
    • Security updates: Weekly maintenance

Base tiers = simplified flat-rate pricing that includes management.

Add-ons = strict cost + 25% on infrastructure, $150/hour for custom work.
