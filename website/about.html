The Story

I'm Jeremy. I run a small software consultancy (intent solutions io) while driving a truck part-time to keep the lights on.

This tweet is exactly why I started CostPlusDB.

"They charge you $280/month. Actual cost? $12. Markup? 2,233%."

Cloud database pricing is absurd. AWS RDS, Google Cloud SQL, and other managed PostgreSQL providers charge 20-30x what the infrastructure actually costs.

I read that tweet and thought: I can do that.

I've spent 20+ years in restaurant operations and systems management, and more recently building AI automation tools and enterprise solutions. I know how to set up infrastructure, automate processes, manage databases, and keep systems running. Why not apply that to PostgreSQL hosting at a fair price?

So I'm building it. See my full work history at jeremylongshore.com.

Why "Cost Plus 25%"?

I could've done 50%. Or 100%. Or the industry standard of 2000%.

But here's the thing: 25% is enough.

  • Covers my time for setup, monitoring, support
  • Pays for automation tooling
  • Lets me save up for our own hardware (see below)
  • Fair for you, sustainable for me

If I wanted to get rich quick, I'd be working at a FAANG company, not driving a truck. This is about building something long-term and honest.

When Prices Change

The 25% markup is fixed forever. But the underlying infrastructure costs can change. Here's our policy:

Phase 1 (Rented VPS - Current)

Prices might increase if:

  • VPS provider raises prices (Contabo/Hetzner/etc)
  • Backup storage costs increase (Wasabi S3)
  • Always at cost + 25%, with 30 days notice and proof

This is temporary. Once we own our servers, this won't happen.

Phase 2 (Owned Hardware - Goal)

Price stability:

  • Your price drops 30-40% when we migrate to owned servers
  • Then it stays flat - No more provider price changes
  • Only datacenter colo fees which rarely change

Longevity rewards (exploring):

  • Working on a system to reward long-term customers with lifetime discounts
  • Early customers who take a risk on this service should get the best prices
  • Details TBD, but loyalty will be rewarded

You Always Know Why

Every invoice shows the exact cost breakdown. If your price changes, you'll see:

  • What changed and why
  • Old cost vs new cost
  • Proof (provider invoice screenshot)
  • 30 days notice before any increase

Goal: Stable, decreasing prices as we grow.

Long-Term Vision: Our Own Hardware

Right now, I rent VPS servers from providers like Contabo and Hetzner. That's where most of your monthly cost goes.

Goal: Within 12-18 months, own our servers in a colocation datacenter.

The Plan

Phase 1: Rent VPS (Current - Month 0-12)
  • Use Contabo/Hetzner/DigitalOcean/AWS
  • Build customer base
  • Refine operations and automation
  • Save 25% revenue for hardware fund
  • Document everything (you can see our SOPs!)

Status: ✅ Active now

Phase 2: Buy First Server (Month 12-18)
  • Purchase 2x dedicated servers (Dell/HP/Supermicro)
  • Colocation in US datacenter (likely Chicago or Virginia)
  • 10Gbps network, redundant power
  • Cost: ~$8,000 upfront + $200/month colo fees
  • Capacity: 50-100 databases

Your benefit: Monthly price drops 30-40%

Timeline: Q2-Q3 2026

Phase 3: Multi-Region (Month 18-36)
  • Add second datacenter (EU or Asia)
  • Offer high-availability across regions
  • Still 25% markup, just on lower hardware costs

Timeline: 2027

Why Tell You This?

Transparency. You're trusting me with your database. You deserve to know the plan.

When we switch to owned hardware:

  1. Your price goes down (same 25% markup, lower costs)
  2. You see the new breakdown on your invoice
  3. No service interruption (we migrate during your maintenance window)
  4. You get better performance (dedicated hardware vs shared VPS)

See our full business plan for financial projections.

Launch Strategy: 5 Customers Max (Initially)

I'm starting with only 5 customers.

Not because I can't handle more. Because execution is everything to me, and I will not sacrifice quality for quantity or speed.

Why Only 5?

  • Learn from each customer - Refine processes, catch issues early
  • Maintain my 4-hour support SLA - I actually respond to emails
  • Build reputation on excellence - 5 happy customers beats 50 mediocre experiences
  • Solo operator reality - I do setup, support, and monitoring myself

If we're at capacity when you apply: I'll add you to the waitlist. You'll get a slot in 2-4 weeks as I expand carefully.

Building this right is more important than building it fast.

Expansion Plan

  • Month 0-3: Max 5 customers (learn and refine)
  • Month 4-6: Expand to 10-15 customers
  • Month 7-12: Expand to 25-50 customers
  • Month 12+: Scale carefully with automation

Who This Is For

Here's who I'm looking for in those first 5 customers:

Good Fit

  • Indie developers - Building SaaS, tools, or side projects
  • Bootstrapped startups - Watching burn rate, need fair pricing
  • Small businesses - Need a database, not enterprise complexity
  • People who value transparency - Want to see actual costs, not just marketing

Not a Good Fit (Yet)

  • Enterprise SLA requirements - I'm targeting 99.9%, not 99.999%
  • Phone support requirements - I offer email/Slack only (24/7 for critical issues, 30-min response during business hours). Slack upgrade: +$29/mo
  • Complex compliance needs - HIPAA/SOC2 possible later, not at launch

I'm a solo operator building this alongside my consultancy work. That means careful, deliberate growth.

Easy Exit Strategy

You're never trapped. Here's how easy it is to leave:

Export Your Data (Anytime)

  • Full database dump - pg_dump provided on request (or you can run it yourself)
  • Direct database access - You have superuser credentials, export whenever you want
  • Backup access - Download any of your backups from the past 30 days

Migration Assistance

  • Migration guide - Step-by-step instructions to move to AWS/GCP/self-hosted
  • Connection string format - Standard PostgreSQL, works with any provider
  • No lock-in - No proprietary extensions or custom configurations
  • Keep database running - We'll keep it live during your migration (no rush)

Cancellation

  • No contracts - Cancel anytime with 7 days notice
  • Prorated refund - Unused days refunded automatically
  • Data retained 30 days - In case you need to come back or forgot something
  • No hard feelings - Seriously. I'd rather you leave happy than stay unhappy.

Your data is YOUR data. I'm just hosting it.
