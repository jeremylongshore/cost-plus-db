Solution: Transparent cost-plus pricing

Every month you get an invoice showing:

  1. VPS cost (Contabo/Hetzner/DO/AWS - your choice)
  2. Backup cost (Wasabi S3 or Cloudflare R2)
  3. Monitoring cost (Betterstack/UptimeRobot)
  4. Total infrastructure cost
  5. Our 25% markup
  6. Your monthly price

If costs go down, your price goes down. If costs go up, we email you first.

Long-term: We're buying our own hardware

Goal: Own colocation servers within 12-18 months.
Why: Lower costs for you, better margins for us.
Plan: Start with 2x dedicated servers in US datacenter

When we make this switch:

  • You see the new cost breakdown on your invoice
  • No interruption to your service
  • Still 25% markup, just on lower costs
